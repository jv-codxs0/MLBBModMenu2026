    Can I get banned?

    Modified game files can break the game’s terms of service. Accounts that use them may be restricted or banned, so don’t use an account you care about.

    The download didn’t start. What now?

    Check that you have about 300 MB free, then press the button again. If your browser blocks it, allow downloads for this page.

    How do I extract a ZIP on Android?
